UK Crude Import Analysis
Only 8% of UK crude transits Hormuz. All Mediterranean supply converges at Gibraltar before reaching UK refineries.
Norway and the United States supply 53% of UK crude with no major maritime chokepoint. Gulf crude (Saudi Arabia and Iraq, 8% combined) exits Hormuz, transits Bab el-Mandeb and Suez, and reaches the UK via Gibraltar. Kazakhstan routes via the CPC pipeline to Novorossiysk and the Bosphorus, emerging into the Mediterranean and clearing Gibraltar. Libya and Algeria load directly into the Mediterranean and also clear Gibraltar. North Sea and Atlantic flows bypass the entire chain. Hover any node or flow for detail.
