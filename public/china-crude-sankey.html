Crude Import Analysis
The 80% figure bundles two different things. Only one of them closes with Malacca.
Gulf crude exits Hormuz, crosses the Indian Ocean, and transits Malacca - two sequential chokepoints. West African and Brazilian VLCCs round the Cape of Good Hope and enter China via Lombok, bypassing Malacca entirely. Russian ESPO loads at Kozmino and crosses the Pacific: no chokepoint. The structural Malacca dependency is 55.8%, not 80%. Hover for detail.
