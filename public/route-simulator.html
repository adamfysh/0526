Northwest Passage and the Bering route are always excluded as non-commercial — not offered as toggles. Closing a chokepoint here removes it entirely from the routing graph and recomputes the shortest remaining path. Cape of Good Hope / Cape Horn are the two around-the-continent detour routes rather than fixed infrastructure — closing one simulates that detour also being unavailable (e.g. weather, piracy), stacking with the canal/strait closures above. Danish Straits and Dover/the English Channel aren't offered yet — the underlying routing graph doesn't tag them as distinct passages, only as open water, so there's nothing there to block until that's built.
Set an origin, destination, and any closures, then compute a route.
Routes are computed live against a real global maritime routing graph (9,708 nodes / ~16,000 edges) — the same network searoute-py uses internally, not a simplified approximation. Distances and durations are estimates for exploration, not for voyage planning.
