UK LNG Import Analysis
Qatar (27%) clears Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb, Suez, and Gibraltar to reach the UK. The Americas now supply 47% with no chokepoint at all.
The UK LNG supply mix has been transformed by US liquefaction capacity. Americas cargoes (USA, Trinidad, others) reach South Hook and Isle of Grain via the North Atlantic with no chokepoint exposure. Norway and Russia supply the North Sea corridor directly. Qatari cargoes, which underpin long-term UK gas contracts, must clear four successive chokepoints: Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb, Suez Canal, and Gibraltar.
